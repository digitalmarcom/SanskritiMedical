How to Reach Us

By Road

Located on NH-19 (Delhi–Agra highway), 25 km from Mathura city and 45 km from Agra. Approachable from Delhi-NCR via Yamuna Expressway.

By Rail

Nearest railway station: Mathura Junction (25 km). Auto/Cab readily available from station to campus.
