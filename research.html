Research Overview

Research at Sanskriti Santosh Medical College & Research Centre is an integral part of the institutional mission. We are committed to building a culture of scientific inquiry, evidence-based practice, and ethical research among faculty, postgraduates, and undergraduate MBBS students.

The Research Cell coordinates all academic research activities including student research projects (SRPs), faculty-led clinical and basic science research, grant applications, and publication support. The Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) oversees the ethical conduct of all human studies.

Student Research Projects

Mandatory Short Research Projects (SRP) for MBBS students in Phase 2 — as per the CBME curriculum. Mentored by faculty researchers.

Faculty Research

Departmental research committees in all 13 clinical and preclinical departments coordinate ongoing faculty-led research projects.

Journal Club

Monthly journal clubs in each department to promote critical appraisal of literature among students and faculty.

Biostatistics Support

Community Medicine department provides biostatistics guidance and SPSS/statistical support for research scholars.

Research Grants

Faculty encouraged to apply for ICMR, DBT, DST, and other government and private research grants. Institutional support provided.

Publication Support

Library and Research Cell provide guidance on manuscript preparation, journal selection, and submission process.

Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC)

All research involving human participants at SSMCRC must receive prior approval from the Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) as mandated by ICMR National Ethical Guidelines.

The Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) of SSMCRC is constituted and operates as per the ICMR National Ethical Guidelines for Biomedical and Health Research involving Human Participants (2017) and the Good Clinical Practice (GCP) guidelines.

Composition

  • Chairperson (external, non-medical)
  • Clinicians from major departments
  • Basic scientists
  • Legal expert
  • Social scientist / patient representative
  • Member Secretary

Mandate

  • Review all human research proposals
  • Ensure informed consent compliance
  • Monitor ongoing research studies
  • Review Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)
  • Annual review of approved studies

Scope of Review

  • Clinical trials (Phase I–IV)
  • Observational studies (case-control, cohort)
  • Student research projects (SRPs)
  • Case reports and series
  • Biobank sample collection studies

Submission Process

  • Download IEC Application Form
  • Submit with complete protocol
  • IEC meets monthly (2nd Wednesday)
  • Decision communicated within 30 days
  • CTRI registration guidance available

Key Research Areas

Clinical Medicine

Communicable and non-communicable diseases, metabolic disorders, tropical diseases, and outcomes research in General Medicine.

Surgical Sciences

Minimally invasive surgery, laparoscopic procedures, surgical outcomes, wound healing, and gastrointestinal surgery.

Maternal & Child Health

High-risk pregnancy outcomes, neonatal morbidity/mortality, child nutrition, and paediatric infections.

Infectious Diseases

Antimicrobial resistance, TB diagnostics, vector-borne diseases, and hospital infection surveillance.

Community & Public Health

Epidemiology, health promotion, social determinants of health, and rural health outcomes in Mathura region.

Medical Education Research

CBME implementation, assessment validity, student competency, and faculty development effectiveness studies.

Conferences, CMEs & Workshops

SSMCRC regularly organizes and participates in medical conferences, Continuing Medical Education (CME) programs, and skill-development workshops.

Annual Medical Conference

Departmental and inter-departmental scientific conferences with paper and poster presentations by faculty and students.

CME Programs

Regular CME sessions on recent advances in each specialty. External faculty speakers from premier institutions invited.

Skill Workshops

Hands-on workshops on surgical skills, ultrasound, advanced life support (ACLS/ATLS), endoscopy, and research methodology.

Student Research Day

Annual research day for MBBS student research project presentations — judged by external experts. Best research awarded.

Medical Education Unit (MEU)

The Medical Education Unit at SSMCRC drives curriculum reform, assessment innovation, and faculty development — ensuring SSMCRC stays at the cutting edge of medical education in India.

Faculty Development Programs

Basic and Advanced Courses in Medical Education (BCME / ACME). Internal and external training for faculty.

Curriculum Monitoring

Regular curriculum review meetings, competency mapping, and student performance tracking for CBME compliance.

Assessment Research

Developing validated assessment tools, OSCE station libraries, and structured clinical examination frameworks.
