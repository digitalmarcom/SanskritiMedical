About the Institution

Sanskriti Santosh Medical College & Research Centre (SSMCRC) is a recognized institution established under the aegis of the Sanskriti Educational Trust. Located strategically on 28 K. M. Stone, Mathura - Delhi Highway, Mathura (U.P.), (INDIA), Uttar Pradesh, the college is situated in an easily accessible location for students from across North India.

The institution operates its fully attached teaching hospital — Sanskriti Chikitsalaya — a 464-bed multi-speciality hospital that serves as the primary site for clinical training. The hospital provides real-world medical exposure across 13 clinical departments, 26 wards, and specialized units including ICU, SICU, MICU, BICU, and HDU.

SSMCRC is approved by the National Medical Commission (NMC), Government of India, and is affiliated to Uttar Pradesh University of Medical Sciences (UPUMS), Saifai, Etawah — the apex medical university of Uttar Pradesh. Our MBBS program follows the latest Competency-Based Medical Education (CBME) curriculum mandated by the NMC.

About Sanskriti Chikitsalaya

Sanskriti Chikitsalaya is the attached multi-speciality teaching hospital of SSMCRC. Spread across a large campus, the hospital is equipped with state-of-the-art infrastructure designed to deliver both quality patient care and comprehensive clinical training.

464 Teaching Beds

Including 84 ICU beds, 44 HDU beds, and 128 motorized Semi-Fowler beds across all departments.

Modular OTs

Fully equipped, laminar-flow Operation Theatres with modern anesthesia workstations and HD imaging.

24×7 Emergency

Round-the-clock Emergency Department with 30 beds and dedicated trauma bay — fully equipped for mass casualty.

NABL Diagnostics

Full-service diagnostic lab covering Biochemistry, Microbiology, Haematology, Pathology, and Radiology.

NACO Blood Bank

NACO-compliant, 24-hour blood bank with component separation and storage facilities.

ALS Ambulances

Advanced Life Support ambulances with trained paramedics for inter-facility patient transfer and emergency response.
