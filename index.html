About Us

Providing Quality Medical Education Since Foundation

Sanskriti Santosh Medical College & Research Centre is an institution of the Sanskriti Educational Trust, situated on 28 K. M. Stone, Mathura - Delhi Highway, Mathura (U.P.), (INDIA) — one of North India's most historically significant locations. We operate under Sanskriti Chikitsalaya, a fully equipped, 464-bed multi-speciality teaching hospital.

Our MBBS program is recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC) and affiliated to Uttar Pradesh University of Medical Sciences (UPUMS). We offer an environment where academic rigour meets compassionate patient care.

  • NMC-approved with UPUMS affiliation — Government-recognized program
  • 13 clinical departments across Pre-Clinical, Para-Clinical, and Clinical streams
  • Dedicated Medical Education Unit with modern simulation labs
  • Anti-Ragging, Ethics, Grievance committees — safe campus for all
