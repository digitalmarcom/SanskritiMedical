Campus Overview

The SSMCRC campus is located on National Highway 19 (NH-19) in Chhata, Mathura — strategically positioned for accessibility from Mathura, Agra, and Delhi-NCR. The campus integrates all academic, clinical, residential, and recreational facilities within a self-contained, green, and secure environment.

The campus houses the College Academic Block, Sanskriti Chikitsalaya (464-bed hospital), Central Library, multiple laboratory blocks, lecture theatres, boys and girls hostels, intern hostel, cafeteria, sports complex, auditorium, and administrative offices — all within easy walking distance.

Location

NH-19, Chhata, Mathura, UP — well-connected by road from Mathura, Agra, Hathras, and Aligarh.

Green Campus

Tree-lined avenues, landscaped gardens, solar lighting, and rainwater harvesting — an eco-conscious campus.

Security

24×7 CCTV surveillance, biometric access, security personnel, and a controlled entry system.

Connectivity

High-speed internet (optical fibre), Wi-Fi across academic and hostel blocks, and digital campus backbone.

Academic Block

The Academic Block houses all pre-clinical and para-clinical departments including Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, Forensic Medicine, and Community Medicine — along with their respective laboratories and faculty offices.

  • Ground to 3rd floor — Department-wise blocks
  • Faculty cabins, departmental offices
  • Conference rooms for tutorials and seminars
  • Students common room and washroom facilities

Central Library

The Central Library at SSMCRC is a fully air-conditioned, spacious reading and reference facility serving students, faculty, and researchers. It houses an extensive collection of medical textbooks, atlases, journals, and digital databases.

Books & Journals

5,000+ medical textbooks and reference books. National and international medical journals — print and online.

Digital Access

Subscriptions to PubMed, Elsevier (ScienceDirect), UpToDate, and e-library portals. Online OPAC system.

Reading Capacity

Separate reading halls with 200+ seating capacity. Individual study carrels with power outlets.

Timings

Monday to Saturday: 8AM – 9PM. Sunday: 10AM – 5PM. 24-hour access during examination periods.

Laboratories (50+ Labs)

SSMCRC has 50+ teaching laboratories across departments — designed for both student practicals and faculty research, equipped with modern instruments and safety infrastructure.

Anatomy Labs

Dissection hall with 10 tables, histology lab with research microscopes, embryology & neuroanatomy museums.

Physiology Labs

Digital spirometers, ECG machines, B.P. recording instruments, computer-based simulations and haematology equipment.

Biochemistry Labs

Spectrophotometers, ELISA readers, PCR machines, electrophoresis systems, and student practical benches.

Pathology Labs

Research microscopes, digital slide scanner, histoprocessing machines, microtome, frozen section facility.

Microbiology Labs

BSL-2 safety cabinets, ELISA systems, autoclaves, anaerobic culture chambers, and molecular diagnostic tools.

Pharmacology Labs

CPCSEA-registered animal facility, drug formulation equipment, computer-aided pharmacology simulations.

Lecture Theatres

Three state-of-the-art, air-conditioned lecture theatres with advanced audio-visual technology serve as the primary venues for didactic teaching sessions.

Main Lecture Theatre

  • 250-seat tiered auditorium-style layout
  • Dual 4K projectors + smart board
  • Dual camera lecture capture system
  • Central air-conditioning

Department Seminar Rooms (×2)

  • 120-seat capacity each
  • Interactive smart boards
  • Video conferencing capability
  • Department-specific seating

Simulation & Skills Centre

The Clinical Skills and Simulation Centre provides a safe, supervised environment for students to practice clinical procedures before performing them on real patients.

High-Fidelity Simulators

Adult and paediatric patient simulators for resuscitation, intubation, ventilator management, and code scenarios.

Clinical Skills Trainers

Suturing trainers, IV cannulation arms, urinary catheterization models, nasogastric tube trainers, and more.

Obstetric Simulators

Birthing simulators for normal delivery, forceps delivery, breach, and PPH management training.

Hostel Facilities

SSMCRC provides separate, well-maintained residential facilities for male students, female students, and intern doctors — offering a safe, comfortable home-away-from-home.

Boys Hostel

  • Single and double-occupancy rooms
  • Attached bathroom
  • Study table, wardrobe, inverter power backup
  • High-speed Wi-Fi, common TV room
  • Laundry facility, housekeeping

Girls Hostel

  • Single and double-occupancy rooms
  • Lady warden on campus 24/7
  • Secure, CCTV-monitored premises
  • Separate recreation room
  • Strict visitor policy for safety

Intern Hostel

  • Dedicated hostel for intern doctors
  • Proximity to hospital for night duties
  • Common kitchen and lounge area
  • Internet, cable TV

Hostel Mess

  • Hygienic, nutritious meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks)
  • North Indian, South Indian, and regional options
  • FSSAI-compliant kitchen
  • Special diet on request

Cafeteria & Food Court

The campus cafeteria is a spacious, modern food court open to all students, faculty, and staff — offering a variety of healthy, affordable meal options in a clean, pleasant environment.

Timings

Monday to Sunday: 7:30 AM – 9:30 PM. Extended hours during examination periods.

Menu Highlights

North Indian thali, sandwiches, pasta, juices, chai, snacks, and regional specials. Special menus on festivals.

Sports & Recreation Facilities

A healthy mind resides in a healthy body. SSMCRC is committed to the physical and mental wellness of its students through comprehensive sports and recreational infrastructure.

Basketball & Volleyball

Flood-lit courts for both sports. Inter-departmental and inter-college tournaments organized regularly.

Football Ground

Full-size football ground with practice goals. Cricket pitch adjacent to the sports ground.

Badminton Courts

Covered, well-lit badminton courts for student and faculty recreation.

Athletics Track

Running track for morning fitness and competitive events during sports week.

Gymnasium

Fully-equipped gymnasium with modern cardio and strength training equipment — open to all students and staff.

Indoor Games

Table tennis, carrom, chess, and indoor board games in the student recreation room.

Auditorium & Conference Hall

The campus has a centrally located air-conditioned auditorium and a dedicated conference hall for academic events, CMEs, cultural programs, and convocations.

Main Auditorium

  • 500-seat capacity
  • Professional AV system and stage lighting
  • Used for Convocation, Cultural Fests, CMEs

Conference Hall

  • 100-seat capacity
  • Video conferencing system
  • Used for workshops, symposia, faculty meetings

Transportation

SSMCRC provides transportation facilities for day-scholar students from Mathura city and surrounding areas to the campus — ensuring safe, convenient commute.

College Buses

Fleet of college buses covering Mathura, Chhata, and nearby areas. AC and non-AC options available.

Hospital Shuttle

Dedicated shuttle between academic block and hospital block for students on clinical postings.

Student Life & Activities

Cultural Events

Annual cultural festival (Sanskriti Utsav), medical quiz, debates, dance, music, and drama competitions.

NSS / Community Service

National Service Scheme unit for health camps, blood donation drives, and rural community outreach.

Anti-Ragging

Strict zero-tolerance anti-ragging policy. 24/7 helpline. Regular sensitization workshops for all students.

Mental Health Cell

Dedicated counselling cell with trained counselors for academic, personal, and psychological support.

Medical Associations

Active MSAI (Medical Students Association), departmental clubs, journal clubs, and research societies.

Women's Cell

Internal Complaints Committee and women's welfare cell ensuring a safe, respectful environment for all.

Modern campus on NH-19, Chhata, Mathura. 50+ labs, central library, sports complex, hostels for boys and girls. NMC-approved MBBS program.
