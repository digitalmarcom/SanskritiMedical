Admission Overview

Sanskriti Santosh Medical College & Research Centre invites applications from eligible candidates for the MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery) programme for the academic session 2026–27. All admissions are conducted strictly as per National Medical Commission (NMC) regulations and UPUMS guidelines.

85%

Government / State Quota

Allotted through UPUMS state counseling based on NEET rank and State Merit List

15%

Management Quota

Institute-level counseling. Direct admission for eligible NEET candidates

NRI

NRI / Overseas Quota

Available for NRI candidates and children of NRIs. Special fee structure applicable

NEET (UG) score is mandatory for ALL quota admissions including Management and NRI. No admission is made without a valid NEET score.

Eligibility Criteria

Nationality

Indian citizens, Overseas Citizens of India (OCI), NRIs, and foreign nationals as per NMC guidelines.

Age Requirement

Candidate must be 17 years old as on 31st December of the year of admission.

Academic Qualification

  • 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board
  • Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, English
  • Minimum 50% aggregate in PCB (40% for reserved categories)

NEET UG Score

Valid NEET (UG) score is mandatory. Minimum qualifying percentile as per NMC norms for each category.

Step-by-Step Admission Process

01

Appear in NEET (UG) Examination

Register and appear in the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET UG) conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). A valid NEET score is mandatory for all categories.

02

Check NEET Result & Rank

After NEET results are declared, check your All-India rank and state rank. Verify eligibility as per the category-wise cut-off communicated by UPUMS / NMC.

03

Register for UPUMS State Counseling (85% Govt Quota)

Register on the official UPUMS counseling portal. Upload required documents, pay counseling fee, and participate in choice-filling and locking process. Seat allotment based on NEET rank and preferences.

04

Management Quota — Apply Directly (15%)

For Management Quota, apply directly at SSMCRC by filling the application form available online or at the admissions office. Submit all required documents and attend document verification.

05

Document Verification

Report to the college with all original documents for verification. Documents will be checked by the admissions committee as per NMC and UPUMS requirements.

06

Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation

Pay the required tuition and other fees as per the fee schedule. Admission is confirmed only after fee payment and final document verification. Receive your Admission Confirmation Letter.

07

Induction & Joining

Attend the mandatory Induction Program (Deeksharambh) as prescribed by NMC. Collect hostel keys, identity card, and other materials. MBBS academic session commences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NEET mandatory for all categories including Management Quota?
Yes. NEET (UG) score is mandatory for all categories — General, OBC, SC, ST, Management Quota, and NRI Quota — as per the National Medical Commission mandate. No admission is granted without a valid NEET score.
What is the intake capacity for MBBS at SSMCRC?
The NMC-approved annual intake for the MBBS program at Sanskriti Santosh Medical College & Research Centre is as per the approved Letter of Permission. Please visit our Statutory Disclosure page for the exact intake numbers.
How is the Management Quota admission conducted?
Management Quota (15% seats) admissions are conducted at the institute level. Eligible NEET-qualified candidates can apply directly to SSMCRC. Selection is based on NEET merit from among applicants. Fee is regulated by the UP Fee Regulatory Committee.
What is the duration of the MBBS program?
The MBBS program is of 5.5 years duration — comprising 4.5 years of academic and clinical training, followed by 1 year of compulsory rotating internship at Sanskriti Chikitsalaya.
Is hostel accommodation mandatory?
Hostel accommodation is optional for domestic students but strongly recommended given the hospital-based learning schedule. It is mandatory for NRI students. Separate hostels are available for boys, girls, and intern doctors.
Is there any reservation of seats for UP domicile students?
85% of seats fall under the State Quota which is filled through UPUMS state counseling. UP domicile students may be eligible for additional reservations within the state quota as per the state government rules.
Can I apply for NRI quota if I am a person of Indian origin settled abroad?
OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) cardholders and children of NRIs may be eligible for the NRI quota. Please contact the admissions office with your specific documentation for eligibility confirmation.
