Hospital Overview

Sanskriti Chikitsalaya is a 464-bed multi-speciality teaching hospital, the primary clinical training ground for MBBS students of SSMCRC. Strategically located on NH-19, Chhata, Mathura, it serves as both a regional healthcare provider and an advanced teaching hospital as mandated by the NMC.

The hospital runs 13 clinical departments, 26 wards, 84 ICU beds, 44 HDU beds, 128 motorized beds, 5 modular operation theatres, a 24/7 emergency department, NABL-accredited diagnostic labs, NACO-compliant blood bank, and round-the-clock pharmacy services.

Emergency Services (24×7)

Sanskriti Chikitsalaya's Emergency Department is operational round the clock with 30 dedicated beds including ICU-level care. The department is equipped to handle medical, surgical, obstetric, paediatric, and trauma emergencies.

Resuscitation Bay

2-bed resuscitation area with crash cart, defibrillator, ventilator, and central monitoring

Trauma Bay

Dedicated trauma management area for road accident victims and polytrauma cases

Obstetric Emergency

24-hour obstetric emergency with dedicated O&G staff and neonatal support

ICU / HDU Services

Medical ICU (MICU)

Multi-parameter monitoring, ventilators, infusion pumps. Managed by critical care specialists 24/7.

Surgical ICU (SICU)

Post-operative intensive care with trained ICU nurses and surgical residents in constant attendance.

Baby ICU (BICU / NICU)

Neonatal and paediatric intensive care with incubators, phototherapy units, and neonatal ventilators.

Emergency HDU

High Dependency Unit adjacent to Emergency for step-down ICU care and close monitoring.

Monitoring & Equipment

Central nurses station with multi-bed monitoring. Latest generation ventilators, CRRT machines, and cardiac monitors.

ICU Team

Dedicated intensivists, ICU-trained nurses, respiratory therapists, and round-the-clock pharmacist support.

Operation Theatres

Sanskriti Chikitsalaya has 5 fully equipped, modular, positive-pressure laminar-flow Operation Theatres designed for all major surgical disciplines.

General Surgery OT (×2)

  • Laparoscopic tower (4K)
  • Major open surgery capability
  • Harmonic scalpel, LigaSure

Orthopaedic OT

  • Image intensifier (C-arm)
  • Arthroscopy tower
  • Fracture fixation instruments

Obstetric OT

  • Dedicated LSCS theatre
  • Neonatal resuscitation trolley
  • Adjacent to labour room

Minor OT / Speciality OT

  • ENT, Ophthalmology, Dermatology
  • Eye microscope, ENT endoscopy
  • Cataract phaco machine

Diagnostics Services

Clinical Pathology & Haematology

Complete blood counts, coagulation studies, peripheral smear, bone marrow biopsy, urinalysis.

Biochemistry

Liver function, kidney function, lipid profile, thyroid function, blood glucose, cardiac enzymes.

Microbiology

Culture & sensitivity, TB diagnostics (CBNAAT), serology, viral markers, rapid diagnostic tests.

Histopathology / Cytology

Biopsy processing, FNAC, frozen section, PAP smear, and immunohistochemistry.

Radiology & Imaging

Digital X-ray, Ultrasound, Colour Doppler, CT Scan. Teleradiology support. 24/7 emergency imaging.

Cardiology Investigations

ECG, 2D Echocardiography, Holter monitoring, TMT (Stress test) available on request.

Pharmacy Services

Sanskriti Chikitsalaya operates a round-the-clock in-house pharmacy stocked with essential medicines, critical care drugs, surgical consumables, and orphan drugs. The pharmacy is managed by qualified pharmacists.

24/7 Operation

Inpatient pharmacy operational round the clock. Night pharmacy counter for emergency medication dispensing.

Drug Formulary

National Essential Medicines List (NLEM) drugs available. Generic medicines provided to eligible patients at subsidized rates.

Blood Bank

The Blood Bank at Sanskriti Chikitsalaya is licensed by NACO (National AIDS Control Organisation) and equipped for whole blood collection, component separation, storage, and crossmatching.

Component Separation

Packed RBCs, Platelets, FFP, and Cryoprecipitate available from donated whole blood.

NACO Licensed

Fully compliant with NBTC guidelines and NABH blood bank standards. Regular internal audits.

24/7 Availability

Emergency blood issue 24 hours. Crossmatch TAT under 45 minutes for emergency requests.

Ambulance Services

Sanskriti Chikitsalaya operates Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulances for emergency patient transport and inter-facility transfer. Staffed by trained paramedics.

Ambulance Helpline

+91-565-228-9998

Patient & Attendant Facilities

Cafeteria

Subsidized canteen for patients and attendants. Hygienic, fresh food available from 7AM to 9PM daily.

Wheelchair & Ramp

Wheelchairs available at entrance. Ramps, lifts, and disability-friendly infrastructure throughout.

Social Work Cell

Medical social workers for financial aid, government scheme enrollment (Ayushman Bharat), and patient counseling.

Parking

Dedicated car and two-wheeler parking for patients and attendants. Free for emergency cases.

Billing & Insurance

Cashless facility for Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY), CGHS, and empanelled insurance. Transparent billing.

Patient Helpdesk

Dedicated helpdesk at entrance for OPD guidance, registration, and query resolution.
